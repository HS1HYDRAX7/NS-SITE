Beyond the Halls

Beyond the Halls is a hyper-realistic horror game inspired by the Backrooms, Dreamcore, and the eerie found-footage style of Kane Pixels. Explore mysterious and endless levels, each filled with unique environments, strange phenomena, and dangerous entities. Every hallway can lead somewhere unexpected, and reality itself may not always behave normally. Explore. Survive. Go beyond the halls.
